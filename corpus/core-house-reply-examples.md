# House Reply Examples (core)

Real replies the TextTonality team has sent. They are the gold standard for voice, rhythm and moves: study them before writing. Match their feel, adapt them to the objection, the client and the client's country, and only reuse a line nearly word for word when it fits the objection exactly. (Dashes have been converted to ellipses or commas: we never send em dashes.)

## House moves: what these replies have in common

- **Casual opener, often lowercase, often with their first name:** "Totally fair {first_name}...", "Totally cool.", "All good....", "No problem.", "No problem at all.", "Got it…", "Sounds good {first_name}...", "Appreciate the reply.", "Totally makes sense, Marc.", "That's excellent." Short and human, then straight to the point.
- **The fork question.** Give them two easy doors: "just curious, is it more of a timing thing… or because you've already got a financial plan dialed in?" / "was that a 'no' because you've already solved for this… or because it sounds too good to be real?"
- **The negative-framed ask.** "would you be opposed to a quick intro call?" plus the soft reason "just so we're not strangers when the timing is right."
- **We're not replacing anyone.** "Full disclosure, not looking to replace your advisor." "I don't replace your CPA, I make them look like a genius." "A second set of eyes on the numbers."
- **Worst case / best case.** "Worst case, you walk away even more confident in the plan you already have. Best case, we spot something small that moves the needle."
- **What would make it worth it?** "Just out of curiosity… what would actually make a tax strategy worth your time to look at?"
- **One specific, intriguing hook, never a list.** "legally recapture taxes you're guaranteed to lose anyway", "shift a chunk of your money from tax-bound to tax-free", "access more of your corporate cash without triggering excess tax".
- **Low-pressure takeaway.** "No pitch, just a quick look. Up to you." "worst case, it confirms you're already dialed in."
- **Small, specific asks.** "15 mins", "a 10-min call", "a quick intro call", "coffee (I see you're also in Albany)". The prospect still picks the time.
- **Ask where it missed.** "was it the strategy that felt off, the timing, or just not something you mess with at all? Always helps to know where the miss was so I don't keep swinging in the dark."
- **Rhythm.** Ellipses for natural pauses, plain words, 2 to 3 short lines, each thought on its own line. Lowercase starts are fine.
